Gift Keeper — Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Gift Keeper is run by Mary Beth (Mary Beth's Finds). Questions: use the Send feedback button inside Gift Keeper.

What Gift Keeper keeps: your email address, and the gift lists you type in — people, gifts, hiding places, dates, and amounts you choose to add. That's it. Your list is saved to your account in the cloud, so it's there when you sign in on any device — not only on one phone.

If you tap "📸 Read a screenshot or label" on the gift form, that one photo goes to Google's Gemini reader, which reads the words on it and hands them back to the form for you to check — nothing is saved until you tap Save gift. The reader never hands back home addresses from a label, and Gift Keeper keeps a fingerprint of the photo plus the words it read, so reading the same photo again is free. The full plain-words version lives on the Privacy page in Settings.

What it does NOT do: no ads, no trackers, and your list is never sold or shared with anyone. Nobody else can see your list — every person's list is private to their own sign-in.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Gift Keeper never sees or stores your card number.

If you want your account and your list deleted, you can do it yourself in Settings — look for "Delete my account" — or send a feedback message inside the app and it will be removed. Deleting does not by itself refund the $15.99 a year, and it doesn't cancel the subscription either — refunds and cancellations are Marybeth's call, as the Terms promise.
