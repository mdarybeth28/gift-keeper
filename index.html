Gift Keeper — Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Gift Keeper is run by Mary Beth (Mary Beth's Finds). Questions: use the Send feedback button inside Gift Keeper.

What Gift Keeper keeps: your email address, and the gift lists you type in — people, gifts, hiding places, dates, and amounts you choose to add. That's it.

What it does NOT do: no ads, no trackers, and your list is never sold or shared with anyone. Nobody else can see your list — every person's list is private to their own sign-in.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Gift Keeper never sees or stores your card number.

If you want your account and your list deleted, send a feedback message inside the app and it will be removed.

Gift Keeper — Terms

Last updated: November 2026

Gift Keeper costs $11.99, paid once. There is no subscription and no ads. Paying gives you your own Gift Keeper account, let in by hand — usually the same day.

Check out with the same email you will sign in with, so your payment and your account match.

Refunds: if Gift Keeper doesn't work for you, ask within 14 days of paying and you'll get your $11.99 back.

Gift Keeper is a helper for your own lists. Keep your sign-in and your PIN to yourself — the PIN lock only works on your own phone.
